The Inspiration

ChandaCore is inspired by Pingala's Chandaśāstra, an ancient Indian work on poetic meter that describes patterns of Laghu (short) and Guru (long) syllables.

These binary-like Laghu–Guru patterns can be explored through combinatorics, enumeration, and algorithmic thinking.

ChandaCore brings these ideas to life through interactive tools that allow students, researchers, and enthusiasts to explore, visualize, and understand the mathematical structure behind patterns.
