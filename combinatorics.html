Key Insights

What combinatorics reveals about Laghu–Guru patterns.

Symmetry

The distribution is symmetric around n/2.

Maximum

The largest number of patterns occurs at k = n/2 (for even n).

Total Count

The sum of all binomial coefficients equals 2ⁿ.

Connection to Probability

The distribution follows a binomial distribution with p = 0.5.
